# SAL-006 -- Heaven: Face to Face at Last

## The claim in plain words

The address that spoke in words, then became a person, reaches its end: presence. "Now we see in a mirror, dimly, but then we will see face to face" (1 Cor 13:12). "He will wipe every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more" (Rev 21:4). "Heaven is the ultimate end and fulfillment of the deepest human longings" (CCC 1024).

The church claims the believer's end is not extinction and not merely relief — it is the address become presence, without veil.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that heaven exists, that "face to face" is a real state, or that communion once given is permanent. It records the claim that the believer's end is full presence — and that this presence is the goal against which the whole life is measured.
