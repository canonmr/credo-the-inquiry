# SAL-004 -- Judgment: Your Life Will Be Weighed

## The claim in plain words

The address that called the believer will also weigh him. "It is appointed for mortals to die once, and after that the judgment" (Heb 9:27). The Son of Man "will separate people one from another as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats" (Matt 25:32) — and the separation is decided by what was done to the least: "I was hungry and you gave me food" (Matt 25:35).

The church claims the weighing happens twice: at death, personally and at once (CCC 1022); at the end of history, for all the nations together.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that the soul is judged, that Matthew 25 foretells a real event, or that deeds are weighed. It records the claim that the believer's life is weighed — a claim about the moment after the last one we can watch.
