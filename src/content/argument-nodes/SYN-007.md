# SYN-007 -- The Integrity Test

## The claim in plain words

An inquiry can be tested by its own honesty: did it quote what weakened it? Did it leave the strongest objections standing? Did it publish its limits before its conclusions?

This inquiry did: the church's own admissions are quoted against its interest (1 Cor 15:19; CCC 1033; LG 16); every ledger has a not_established column; no worldview was scored. The reader can check — and the check is the test.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not award the grade. Honesty of method does not prove correctness of conclusions — it only proves the conclusions were not concealed. The test is open; the verdict is the reader's.
