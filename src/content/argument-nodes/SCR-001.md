# SCR-001 -- Scripture Is a Historical Text

## The claim in plain words

Imagine an old letter found in an attic. Before you believe its contents, you ask: who wrote it, when, from where, to whom? That is the natural treatment of any document. This node only affirms that Scripture deserves the same treatment — not because it is unexceptional, but because its exceptionality must itself be tested honestly.

Luke himself begins his Gospel that way: he writes after "carefully investigating everything from the beginning", on the basis of eyewitness testimony. Scripture does not ask to be exempted from examination; it asks to be read as a book written by human beings — and that is where the examination begins.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that Scripture is true, does not prove that Scripture is inspired, and does not demand that historical method be the only way to read. It only clears one obstacle: the assumption that examining Scripture historically is an insult to it.
