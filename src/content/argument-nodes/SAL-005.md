# SAL-005 -- Hell: The Door Closed From Within

## The claim in plain words

The church claims the address has a limit: it can be finally refused. "Depart from me into the eternal fire" (Matt 25:41). And the Catechism names the state: "definitive self-exclusion from communion with God" (CCC 1033) — chosen, not inflicted. The door is closed from within.

Yet the church limits its own claim: the Council teaches that those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel can still be saved (LG 16). The church names the possibility of separation — and refuses to name who stands in it.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that anyone is separated, that separation is chosen rather than inflicted, or that the two teachings are consistent. It records the claim and its self-imposed boundary.
