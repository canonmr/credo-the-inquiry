# AUT-002 -- Apostolic Succession: A Traceable History

## The claim in plain words

The Catholic church claims: today's bishops sit in the seats of the apostles. Dei Verbum 8 says the Gospel is preserved "by an unending succession of preachers". Irenaeus, around 180, even drew up the list of Rome's bishops from Peter to his own day.

The historical part of the claim is real and examinable. The theological part — that the chain guarantees truth — is a claim of faith. The two must be distinguished.

## What this argument does NOT establish

A traceable chain proves continuity, not truth. Any two-thousand-year-old organization can draw up its line of officeholders. This node separates the two — and hands the question of truth-guarantee to the next node.
