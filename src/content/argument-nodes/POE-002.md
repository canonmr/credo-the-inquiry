# POE-002 — Knowledge Does Not Necessarily Cause the Known Event

## Plain-language statement

Suppose the proposition 'it will rain tomorrow' is true. The fact that it is true does not, by itself, mean that God caused it to rain tomorrow. Knowledge tracks truth; causation produces truth. The two are not the same.

This is one of the simplest disambiguations in philosophy. Its main work is to break the slide from 'God knows X' to 'God made X happen' — a slide that the popular form of the problem of evil often depends on.

## What this argument does NOT establish

POE-002 does not settle what *does* connect God's knowledge to events. It only rules out one specific bad inference. The chapter must later consider whether some other relation (e.g., willing, ordaining, permitting) does the connecting.
