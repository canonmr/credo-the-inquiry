# AUT-008 -- The Ground of Choice: Authority as Service

## The claim in plain words

After all the circles and tests, one fact remains: no argument chooses the magisterium for us. What remains is a choice — but a choice framed by two things. First, Jesus himself gives the measure of authority: "the leader must become like one who serves" (Luke 22:26). Second, faith is a free response (CCC 143) — and trusting a community is part of that response.

So the question is no longer "who interprets?" but "does this authority serve — or rule?" And the answer must be weighed with open eyes, in the presence of evidence, without asking the evidence to choose for us.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that choosing the magisterium is the right decision — and does not prove it wrong. It establishes: the decision is real, personal, and must not be made blind. Honesty demands weighing both directions; decision demands taking one.
