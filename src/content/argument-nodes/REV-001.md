# REV-001 -- Hiddenness Is Not Absence

## The claim in plain words

Imagine you step into a dark, silent house. No lights are on; there is no sound. Are you entitled to conclude that the house is empty? No — the house could be dark and silent because its occupants are asleep, or away, or sitting quietly in the back room. The light you brought is not enough to see the whole house. From the darkness you see, all you can say is: with a flashlight this small, the house looks empty.

The same goes for divine hiddenness. A God who does not show himself to you — or to anyone you know — is a fact about your experience, not a statement about all of reality. Perhaps God is hidden because of his characteristic way of acting; perhaps because the seeing eye itself is not yet ready; perhaps because the hiddenness itself is part of the invitation.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that God exists. It only tidies up one step of logic: there is no automatic path from "not seen" to "does not exist". Anyone who wants to draw that conclusion must bring an additional premise — for example, that all possible evidence is already complete. That premise is the real point of contention.
