# POE-006 — Omnipotence and Possible Worlds

## Plain-language statement

The popular idea of omnipotence is 'the power to do anything'. That idea is incoherent: omnipotence is the power to do all things that are logically possible, not the power to do what involves a contradiction. On this corrected view, there are possible worlds that even God cannot actualize — including, on at least one well-defended analysis, worlds containing free creatures who always freely do what is right.

This is the modal move behind Plantinga's Free Will Defense. It does not say that the actual world's distribution of evil is the best one, or that God has no reason to do better. It only says that the *existence* of moral evil is not, by itself, a logical impossibility given the existence of God.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This argument does not establish that God has reasons for the actual world's evils. It does not establish that the actual world is good on the whole. It only establishes that God and evil are not logically incompatible.
