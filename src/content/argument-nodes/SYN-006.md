# SYN-006 -- The Address as the Turning Point

## The claim in plain words

Every chapter of the inquiry pointed to one hinge: does the hidden God speak? The prophet named the hiddenness: "Truly, you are a God who hides himself" (Isa 45:15). The text names the door: "I am the gate" (John 10:9); "I am standing at the door, knocking" (Rev 3:20).

If the address is real, every later claim is a door that opens. If it is not, the whole inquiry is a study in the psychology of hope. The hinge cannot be settled by the inquiry — it is the point the inquiry faces.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show that the address is real, or that the biblical door is the door. It shows that every chapter depends on the hinge — and that the hinge remains a decision.
