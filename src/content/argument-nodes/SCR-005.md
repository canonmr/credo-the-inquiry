# SCR-005 -- Historical Criticism: Friend, Not Enemy

## The claim in plain words

Christian claims are not claims about the world of ideas — they are about a man in Palestine who was executed and is said to have risen. Claims about events can only be assessed with the tools for events: historical inquiry. Rejecting those tools makes claims immune to questions — and what is immune to questions is not faith but ideology.

Luke opens the path himself: he writes after "carefully investigating everything from the beginning", on the basis of eyewitness testimony. Historical criticism is not a destroyer — it is the tester that makes testimony trustworthy or not.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that the testimony of the books is true. It only rejects one dishonest move: hiding behind "faith" so that historical claims are not examined. The result of examination may support — or oppose — the testimony. Both must be accepted honestly.
