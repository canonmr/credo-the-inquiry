# SAL-003 -- Salvation: From Death to Life

## The claim in plain words

The claim is simpler than the theology around it: the believer is saved. "The wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord" (Rom 6:23); "so that everyone who believes in him may not perish but may have eternal life" (John 3:16).

What work earns is a wage; what love gives is a gift. The church claims the passage is given, not earned; that it begins now; and that nothing the believer does can turn the gift into a wage.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that the believer has passed from death to life, that sin's wage is death, or that eternal life is real beyond hope. It records the claim that the believer is saved — and that the saving is a gift.
