# AUT-007 -- The Test of Fruits: Can We Know Them?

## The claim in plain words

"Every good tree bears good fruit" (Matt 7:17) — a beautiful and honest criterion. But applied to the church's claim of authority, it becomes ambiguous: the Catholic Church reaps the fruits of its saints and its works of charity; other communities reap the fruits of their piety and sacrifice. There is no neutral scale to decide whose fruit weighs more.

The test of fruits can screen out extreme rottenness. It cannot choose between trees that are equally fruitful.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove the church right or bad by its fruits. It affirms: the test of fruits is real but limited — and that the final verdict must not be hidden behind "look at the fruits", because fruits can always be gathered in support of both parties.
