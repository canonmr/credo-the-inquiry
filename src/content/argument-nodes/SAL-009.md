# SAL-009 -- The Strongest Objection

## The claim in plain words

Every claim of this chapter is held by faith (SAL-007). The strongest objection names what that means: the believer's hope is the most comforting and the least checkable claim in the system — and Clifford's rule condemns it: "It is wrong always, everywhere, and for anyone, to believe anything upon insufficient evidence."

The church itself admits the stakes: "If for this life only we have hoped in Christ, we are of all people most to be pitied" (1 Cor 15:19).

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove the hope false, does not refute the church's answer, and does not declare the objection answered. It states the strongest challenge the chapter can raise — and leaves it open, as the strongest objection should be left.
