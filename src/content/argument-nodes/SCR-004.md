# SCR-004 -- Inerrancy: An Unfinished Question

## The claim in plain words

"Scripture is without error" — that sentence sounds firm until you read its full wording in Dei Verbum 11: the books teach "without error that truth which God wanted put into sacred writings for the sake of our salvation". The question is: does "truth" cover troop numbers, kings' ages, and geography — or only what concerns salvation?

Wide reading: everything is without error. Narrow reading: only the truth for salvation; the rest is the clothing of its age. Vatican I called the books "having God as their author" but never fixed the limit. And Scripture itself contains details that differ in several places. This node does not decide — it only records: the question is undecided, and both readings are still contended.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that Scripture contains errors, and does not prove inerrancy false. It affirms something smaller but harder: the claim "without error" has never been fully defined — so it cannot yet be accepted at full price.
