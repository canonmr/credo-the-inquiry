# REV-008 -- Love That Invites, Not Coerces

## The claim in plain words

Have you seen a parent who deliberately hides behind a door, letting their child search? The child cries for a moment — but the parent knows: when the child finds them, that hug will be the warmest of hugs. Seek, and you will find.

This is the idea behind "intended hiddenness". God does not hide because God is not there; God hides *for* something — to make room for the search, for freedom, for love that is not coerced. Isaiah calls God "a God who hides himself". Pascal says: a religion that does not confess that God is hidden is not a true religion.

What is beautiful in this idea is also what makes it vulnerable. It can only be verified from within — by those who find. For those who find nothing, "intended hiddenness" sounds like post-hoc justification. And post-hoc justification never convinces the wounded.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that every hiddenness is justified, or that God truly addresses, or that those who hear nothing are less honest. It shows one smaller thing: that hiddenness and love *can* coexist. Whether they do coexist in real cases — that is a question demanding evidence, not analogy.
