# SYN-005 -- The Bet That Cannot Be Avoided

## The claim in plain words

"You must wager. It is not optional. You are embarked" — Pascal. The refusal to believe is itself a bet: the skeptic who refuses the address lives as if death were the end, and every abstention is a choice of meaning made in silence.

James names the rule: "Our passional nature not only lawfully may, but must, decide an option between propositions, whenever it is a genuine option that cannot by their nature be decided on intellectual grounds."

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show that the believer's bet wins, that abstention is impossible in every sense, or that a forced choice proves either side. It shows that the neutral seat does not exist — the game deals every player a hand.
