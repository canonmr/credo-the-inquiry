# SAL-001 -- Grace: The Address Continues

## The claim in plain words

The church's claim about the believer begins before the believer acts: the address does not stop with Christ. It continues as grace — "the free and undeserved help that God gives us to respond to his call" (CCC 1996); "by grace you have been saved through faith... not the result of works" (Eph 2:8-9).

In the language of this inquiry: the God who spoke in creation, in Scripture, in Christ, is still speaking — and what he speaks to the believer is a gift.

## What this argument does NOT establish

Naming the grace claim does not prove that grace is real, that the believer's help comes from God, or that the address continues. The church claims it; the claim is now on the table for the rest of the chapter to test.
