# SYN-003 -- What Remains Contested

## The claim in plain words

The inquiry names its limits as exactly as its results. What could not be established: whether God is justified in permitting evil; whether God speaks; whether the books carry the address; whether the interpreter holds authority; whether Christ rose; whether grace is given; whether the believer is judged, saved, or separated.

All of it contested. All of it held by faith — "the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen" (Heb 11:1); "if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it with patience" (Rom 8:24-25).

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not settle any contested claim, and does not show the line is permanent. It names the boundary the six chapters actually found — the facts of the claims established, the truth of the claims contested.
