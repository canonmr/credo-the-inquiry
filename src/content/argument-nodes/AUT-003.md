# AUT-003 -- The Magisterium: The Living Teaching Office

## The claim in plain words

When the question 'who has the right to interpret?' is put to Catholicism, the answer is not vague: the magisterium — the Pope and the bishops in communion with him. "The task of giving an authentic interpretation of the Word of God... has been entrusted to the living teaching office of the Church alone" (Dei Verbum 10; see also Catechism 85).

A clear answer. And because it is clear, it moves the question: why should this office be trusted? That is the question of the next nodes.

## What this argument does NOT establish

Naming an authority is not proving an authority. This node explains what the magisterium claims — and hands the claim over for testing. 'Magisterium' must not become an answer repeated, rather than a reason weighed.
