# CHR-007 -- History and Faith: Two Methods, One Person

## The claim in plain words

The same person — Jesus — is examined by two methods. History verifies the man and the execution (Tacitus), dates the earliest creed (1 Cor 15), and stops there. Faith confesses what history cannot show: "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God" (Matt 16:16).

The two do not contradict — they differ in reach. History reaches the foot of the cross; faith reaches beyond it. And that is why the boundary is not a truce: it marks exactly where the decision lies.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not establish that faith's confession is true, and does not establish that history's reconstruction is the whole truth. It establishes the boundary — and with it, the honest position of the decision: between two readings of the same person, both going beyond strict evidence.
