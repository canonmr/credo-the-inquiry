# SYN-001 -- The Pattern: Six Chapters, One Structure

## The claim in plain words

Every chapter of this inquiry ran the same course: the claim was named, its sources were quoted, its strongest objection was spoken, and its status was written in a ledger. And every chapter ended the same way — with a choice, not a proof.

That is itself the first result: the inquiry did not fail to prove; it refused to pretend. Six chapters, one structure: examine everything, score nothing, leave the decisions where they stand.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show that the pattern was the right method, or that "choice" is the true outcome rather than a verdict the method was built to produce. It records what the six chapters did — nothing more.
