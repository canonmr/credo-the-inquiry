# CHR-008 -- The Circle Returns: The Claims Presuppose the Address

## The claim in plain words

Every claim of this chapter stands on a deeper one. Christ is "the Word made flesh" — the address in person (CHR-001). But the address was not proven in chapter 2; the books were not proven in chapter 3; the interpreter was not proven in chapter 4. And yet the church's claims about Christ, the sacraments, and the church stand on all three.

The believer inhabits this structure: it is what faith is. The outsider sees a circle — a building whose floors rest on choices. This node does not break the circle; it draws it.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not establish that the church's claims are false — and does not establish that the circle cannot be entered. It maps the structure: the claims stand together with the address, and the decision between circles — the believer's circle and the outsider's — is the existential question this inquiry has named from the first chapter.
