# SAL-007 -- One Honest Thing: All of This Is Faith

## The claim in plain words

Every claim of this chapter — grace, conversion, salvation, judgment, hell, heaven — concerns what no believer has seen. The church knows and says so: "Faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen" (Heb 11:1).

All of this is held because it is believed, not because it is seen. That is not a hidden flaw — the church confesses it openly. But admitting that something is believed is not proving it.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show that faith is a reliable route to truth, or that the claims held by faith correspond to anything real. It records the church's own confession — and leaves the last things where they stand: believed, not seen, and staked on everything.
