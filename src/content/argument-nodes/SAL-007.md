# SAL-007 -- The Circle in the Last Things

## The claim in plain words

Every claim of this chapter — grace, conversion, salvation, judgment, hell, heaven — concerns what no believer has observed. The church knows this and names it: "Faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen" (Heb 11:1).

The circle is confessed, not concealed: the text is read by the authority, believed by the believer, each inside the same system examined in chapters 3, 4, and 5.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node describes the circle; it does not open it. It does not show that faith is a reliable route to truth, or that the claims held by faith correspond to anything real. The last things remain the place where the claims are kept beyond every check — and staked on everything.
