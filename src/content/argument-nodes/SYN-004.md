# SYN-004 -- The Circle and the Leap

## The claim in plain words

Every worldview is a circle: it rests on a foundation it cannot demonstrate. The skeptic trusts reason — and cannot prove reason from outside reason. The scientist trusts method — and cannot prove method by another method. The believer trusts the address — and the address is the very thing examined.

The difference is not proof; it is honesty. Kierkegaard names what remains: "an objective uncertainty held fast in an appropriation-process of the most passionate inwardness" — the leap.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show that any leap lands in truth, or that all circles are equally rational. It shows the circles, names the leap — and leaves the leap to the leaper.
