# CHR-001 -- The Central Claim: The Address Made Flesh

## The claim in plain words

Every community stands on a claim. The church stands on this one: Jesus is the Christ, the Son of the living God — the Word made flesh. "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God" (Matt 16:16); "And the Word became flesh and lived among us" (John 1:14); "In these last days he has spoken to us by a Son" (Heb 1:2).

In the language of this inquiry: if God's address to humanity is real, then in Jesus that address became a person. The claim is breathtaking — and it must be examined, not repeated.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node records the claim and its textual form. It does not prove that Jesus is the Christ, and does not prove that the confession is a historical event. Naming a claim is the beginning of examination — not the end of it.
