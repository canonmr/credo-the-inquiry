# SCR-008 -- Reading Honestly

## The claim in plain words

If we are to keep reading these books, how do we do it without deceiving ourselves? Dei Verbum 12 gives two rules. First: ask what the author meant — language, age, genre. Second: read every passage "in the same Spirit in which it was written" — in the light of the whole of Scripture. 2 Peter 1:20-21 affirms: no prophecy is a matter of one's own interpretation; it came from the Holy Spirit.

The two rules protect against two equally fatal errors: reading Scripture like a modern science book, then condemning it for being unscientific; and drawing conclusions from isolated verses without context.

## What this argument does NOT establish

The second rule borrows a commitment: "the same Spirit" can only be held by someone who already believes. This node establishes the discipline honestly — but it does not hide that half the discipline stands on a decision of faith.
