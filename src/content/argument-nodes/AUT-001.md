# AUT-001 -- The Church Claims Authority from Christ

## The claim in plain words

Imagine an organization claiming: "We received this authority from our founder himself." The first question is not whether the claim is true, but whether it has a basis. For the Catholic church, the basis is textual: "You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church" (Matt 16:18); "Whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven" (Matt 18:18); "If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven" (John 20:23).

The church does not claim without a basis. It claims with a basis — and that basis is what is debated.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that the Catholic reading of these texts is right. It only records that the claim is rooted in texts recognized by both sides — and that the difference lies in interpretation. That leads to the next question: who has the right to interpret?
