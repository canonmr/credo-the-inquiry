# SAL-002 -- Conversion: The Turning

## The claim in plain words

The address does not end with a gift; it ends with a demand. "Repent, and believe in the good news" (Mark 1:15); "Repent therefore, and turn to God so that your sins may be wiped out" (Acts 3:19).

The church claims the believer's whole life is shaped by this turning — not one event only, but the direction of everything after it. And the turning, says the church, is enabled by the very grace it answers.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that anyone has turned, that repentance wipes out sins, or that the command is heard as grace. It records what the church says the address requires — nothing more.
