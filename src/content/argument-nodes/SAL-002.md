# SAL-002 -- Conversion: The Turning

## The claim in plain words

The address does not end with a gift; it ends with a demand. "Repent, and believe in the good news" (Mark 1:15); "Repent therefore, and turn to God so that your sins may be wiped out" (Acts 3:19).

The church claims the believer's whole life is shaped by this turning: not a single event only, but the direction of every event after it. And the church claims the turning is enabled by the grace it answers to.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that any believer has turned, that repentance wipes out sins, or that the command is heard as grace. It records what the church claims the address requires — nothing more.
