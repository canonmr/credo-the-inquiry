# SYN-009 -- The Open Door: What the Inquirer Faces

## The claim in plain words

The inquiry ends where it began: before a question the evidence cannot settle — and the church's texts name what stands there. "Enter through the narrow gate" (Matt 7:13-14). "I am standing at the door, knocking" (Rev 3:20). "Come to me, all you that are weary" (Matt 11:28-30). "You have made us for yourself, and our heart is restless until it rests in you" (Augustine, Confessions 1.1.1).

The door is open — not because the inquiry proved it, but because the inquiry could not close it. The knock is waiting. The choice is the inquirer's.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove the address real, the images true, or the door anything more than the church's own image. It states the last word of the inquiry: the examination is complete, the decision is not made — and that is the honest end.
