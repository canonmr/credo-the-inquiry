# SCR-007 -- The Canon–Church Circle

## The claim in plain words

Who chose the 27 books of the New Testament? The church. And what do those books say about the church? That the church is guided by the apostles and their successors. So: the church endorses the books that endorse the church. That is a circle — and there is no smooth way out.

That does not mean the circle must stop us: all human knowledge is rooted in trust in communities. But honesty demands we name it: each side endorses the other, and the final decision is a choice to trust the inheriting community — or not.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove the church wrong or the books false. It proves something smaller but more disturbing: that there is no neutral starting point, and that the claim "the books must be right because the church chose them" is a circle to be borne — not a clean entrance.
