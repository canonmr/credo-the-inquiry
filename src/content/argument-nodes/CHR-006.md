# CHR-006 -- The Marks of the Church: One, Holy, Catholic, Apostolic

## The claim in plain words

"We believe in one holy catholic and apostolic Church" — so confesses the creed of 381. Four words: the church is one, holy, catholic, apostolic.

Now the facts: the visible church is divided into thousands of denominations; its history contains crusades, inquisitions, and scandals; its 'one' is not visibly one, its 'holy' is not visibly holy. The church's answer: the marks describe what the church is from Christ — an identity confessed, not a record of performance. The observer's answer: the marks are contradicted by the facts. Both readings exist. This node does not decide between them — it states the claim and the challenge.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not establish that the church is visibly one or holy — and does not establish that the marks are false. It establishes that the marks are a claim of faith facing a historical challenge, and that the two readings — 'not yet' and 'not so' — are both held honestly. Deciding between them is deciding what the church is.
