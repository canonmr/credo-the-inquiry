# SYN-008 -- The Objection: Choice Is Not Evidence

## The claim in plain words

The strongest objection to the whole inquiry: after six chapters, the church's central claims are still contested — and the inquiry's answer, 'it is a choice,' is not a result but a surrender. A leap is not a reason; a bet is not a proof. Clifford's rule stands: "It is wrong always, everywhere, and for anyone, to believe anything upon insufficient evidence."

The church's answer is strong: no worldview offers a demonstration; the skeptic's position is the same choice, made silently. But the objection remains: naming the choice does not prove it — and the chapter leaves that challenge open, as it has in every chapter before.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show the church's claims false, does not show choice dishonest, and does not award the debate. It states the objection that the whole inquiry must face — and leaves it facing it.
