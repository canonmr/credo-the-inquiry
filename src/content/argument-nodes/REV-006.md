# REV-006 -- God Speaks in History: Special Revelation

## The claim in plain words

There is a big difference between saying "nature points to God" and saying "God spoke to me". The first can be inferred from the stars; the second comes from within history. And that is where the Christian claim stands.

Hebrews 1:1-2 sums it up: long ago God spoke through the prophets — in various ways — and in these last days he spoke through his Son. Vatican II described it in warm language: the invisible God "speaks to men as friends". This is not a metaphor of the heavens opening; it is a claim about specific people, specific words, specific events.

This claim is bold, and because it is bold it is also vulnerable — and that is exactly what makes it interesting. It does not ask us to believe without evidence: it points to texts that can be read, events that can be examined, testimony that can be weighed. An honest person may open Scripture the way one opens any historical document, and ask: can this testimony be trusted?

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that special revelation actually occurs. It only establishes the status of the claim: it is a historical claim that *can* be examined — not an immune card to be accepted without question. Whether the examination yields an answer is the work of the following chapters.
