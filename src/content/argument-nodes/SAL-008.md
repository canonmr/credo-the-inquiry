# SAL-008 -- The Moral Stakes: A Life Changed

## The claim in plain words

The church does not leave the believer's end as a blind belief: it points to the life. "Faith by itself, if it has no works, is dead" (James 2:17). "The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, generosity, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control" (Gal 5:22-23). "You will know them by their fruits" (Matt 7:16).

The church claims the address changes what a life looks like — and invites the inquirer to look.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not prove that the fruit is present, that it is caused by the belief, or that changed lives prove the address real. It records the church's own test — and notes that the test can fail, and has.
