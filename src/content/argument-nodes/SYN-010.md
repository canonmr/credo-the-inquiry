# SYN-010 -- The Epistemic Tension: The Leap and Vatican I

## The claim in plain words

Six chapters weigh evidence; the close names a leap and a bet. The Church, since Vatican I, teaches that the assent of faith is "by no means a blind movement of the mind" — God is known with certainty by natural reason from creation (Rom 1:20), and the obedience of faith is joined to external signs. The tension is real as a matter of reading, and this inquiry marks it.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This node does not show that one reading is certainly right and the other certainly wrong, that Kierkegaard's leap is the fideism Vatican I condemned, or that the Church denies the will's role in faith. It shows that the close of this chapter shifts register — and that the shift is now marked.
