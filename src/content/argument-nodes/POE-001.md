# POE-001 — Foreknowledge Does Not Equal Causation

## Plain-language statement

If I know that the sun will rise tomorrow, my knowing does not make the sun rise. Knowledge and event are distinct, and there is no reason to suppose the first produces the second.

This sounds obvious, but the obviousness is misleading. The moment the knower is infallible, or the moment the knowledge is held to be grounded in something other than the event, the picture changes. POE-001 is the modest disambiguation that anchors the rest of the chapter: it does not solve the deeper problem, it just names a distinction.

## What this argument does NOT establish

This argument does not establish that God has foreknowledge. It does not establish that foreknowledge is compatible with human freedom. It only establishes the conceptual distinction between knowing an event and causing it — a distinction that the rest of the chapter will press on.
